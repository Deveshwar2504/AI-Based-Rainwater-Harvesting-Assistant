RAINWATER HARVESTING MAINTENANCE

Important maintenance practices include:

1. Keep the roof and catchment area clean.
2. Clean gutters and conveyance pipes.
3. Maintain the first-flush arrangement.
4. Clean or replace filter materials when necessary.
5. Inspect storage tanks periodically.
6. Ensure overflow arrangements are functional.
7. Inspect recharge structures regularly.

Before the monsoon season, catchment areas and system components should be
checked and cleaned.

Rainwater harvesting systems should be designed and maintained according
to applicable local guidelines and site conditions.
