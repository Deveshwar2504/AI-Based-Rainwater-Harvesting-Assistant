RAINWATER HARVESTING BASICS

Rainwater harvesting is the collection and management of rainwater for storage,
reuse, or groundwater recharge.

Rooftop rainwater harvesting uses the roof as a catchment area. Rainwater is
collected through gutters and pipes and can be directed to a storage tank or
a groundwater recharge structure.

A basic rooftop rainwater harvesting system can include:

1. Catchment area
2. Gutters and conveyance pipes
3. First-flush arrangement
4. Filter
5. Storage tank or recharge structure
6. Overflow arrangement

Rainwater harvesting can help conserve water and reduce dependence on
conventional water sources.
