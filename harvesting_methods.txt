RAINWATER HARVESTING METHODS

There are two main approaches:

1. Storage for direct use
2. Groundwater recharge

In storage-based harvesting, water collected from the roof is directed to a
storage tank.

In groundwater recharge, collected rainwater is directed to suitable recharge
structures.

The choice between storage and recharge depends on:

- Rainfall
- Roof area
- Water demand
- Available space
- Soil and groundwater conditions
- Local regulations
- Site conditions

A rainwater harvesting system should include appropriate filtration and
overflow arrangements.
